Our Services

New Home Construction

South Coast Builders brings dreams to life by constructing custom homes from the ground up. We work closely with our clients to turn their visions into reality. From initial design concepts to final touches, we work to ensure each new home reflects the unique style and needs of its owners.

Home Additions

For homeowners looking to expand their living space without the hassle of moving, South Coast Builders provides expert home addition services. Whether it's adding a sunroom, extending a kitchen, or building a new wing, our team collaborates closely with clients to seamlessly integrate new spaces into existing structures, ensuring a cohesive and functional result.

Home Remodeling

We understand that a well-executed remodel can breathe new life into a home. From updating kitchens and bathrooms to transforming entire interiors, we approach each remodeling project with precision and creativity. Whether it's modernizing outdated features or maximizing space efficiency, South Coast Builders strives to exceed expectations while staying true to the client's vision.

Custom Cabinets & Millwork

With a passion for fine craftsmanship, South Coast Builders offers bespoke millwork and cabinetry services to elevate any space. Whether it's intricate trim work, custom-built furniture, or handcrafted cabinets, we blend traditional techniques with modern innovation to create stunning and functional pieces that enhance the beauty and functionality of any home or commercial space.

Commercial Trim

Beyond residential projects, South Coast Builders also caters to commercial clients with their expert trim services. From offices to retail spaces, our team provides professional installation of trim and molding to add architectural interest and sophistication to any commercial environment.

Get Started

Ready to bring your vision to life? Contact us today to discuss your project goals and learn how South Coast Builders can turn them into reality. Whether you're planning a residential renovation or a commercial construction project, we're here to provide the expertise and support you need every step of the way.
